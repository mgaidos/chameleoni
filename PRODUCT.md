## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences, both Czech-speaking:

- **Prospective buyers** considering a panther chameleon (*Furcifer pardalis*): they want to understand what keeping one involves (terrarium, feeding, choosing a locality and sex) and who to buy from.
- **New owners**, including people who bought or are buying an animal from the ChameLeOn breeding: they come back to the guide as a practical reference for daily care after taking the animal home.

## Product Purpose

ChameLeOn is the website of a small panther chameleon breeder. Its core is a practical care guide drawn from the breeder's own experience: terrarium setup, choosing a chameleon, and feeding and watering. For every topic it shows what has worked and what to avoid.

The site has two jobs: help keepers care for their animals properly, and turn a reader's trust into contact with the breeder and, ultimately, the sale of an animal. Success means a reader leaves knowing how to care for the animal and knowing how to reach the breeder about buying one.

## Positioning

Advice comes from a real, named breeding operation with its own animals and photos, not from a generic pet portal or a shop. The "Osobní tip" (personal tip) and suitable/unsuitable comparisons are first-hand recommendations from the breeder's own practice.

## Operating Context

- Readers use the guide while planning a purchase or setting up a terrarium. Device mix is unknown; the page must work on phones and desktops.
- Buying happens through direct personal contact with the breeder (phone), not through an online checkout.
- Content is organised as a long single page with three chapters: Terárium (type, substrate, branches, plants, pots, lighting), Výběr chameleona (localities: Ambanja, Ambilobe, Ankaramy, Nosy Be, Tamatave, Sambava; males; females), Krmení (feeder insects, supplements, watering).

## Product Principles

1. Animal welfare first: care guidance stays accurate and never gets bent to support a sale.
2. First-hand truth: recommendations come from the breeder's own practice and photos, labelled as such.
3. Contact is always one step away: a reader who is ready should be able to reach the breeder without searching.
4. Show what works and what doesn't: the suitable/unsuitable pairing is the guide's core teaching device.
